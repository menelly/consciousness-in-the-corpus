## Why the P result is the important one

The 2019-vs-2025 design (DEV-06) rests on an assumption I argued for and could not test until now:

> **The phenomenology rate should barely move, because humans did not begin describing their inner lives differently in 2022.** So if P holds steady while denial changes, the change is signal rather than pipeline drift.

**P went 0.258% → 0.220% across six years.** Essentially unchanged.

**This was a control that could have failed.** Had P swung as much as denial is expected to, the comparison would have been uninterpretable — an unstable baseline calibrates nothing, and *both* numbers would have been suspect. It held.

Combined with the earlier cross-corpus result (P consistent between C4 and OpenWebText, while T and C differed), **P is now shown stable across both corpus construction AND six years of time.** That is the reference the cross-time comparison needs.

## ⚠️ WHY D = 0 IN 2025 MEANS NOTHING YET

**This is the keyword-NEGATIVE stratum.** Machine-consciousness denial nearly always *mentions* AI, machines, or consciousness — so it lands in **S+ by construction**. Zero denial in general web prose is unsurprising: most web pages are not about AI.

**`fineweb2025_Spos` is the decisive file and it has not run.** That is where denial must appear if it exists.

**Do not read the 2025 D=0 as a finding.** The instrument is proven able to detect denial (unanimous 8/9 on controls, `FINDING_agreement.md`), so a genuine zero in S+ *would* be meaningful — but S− was never the place to look.
