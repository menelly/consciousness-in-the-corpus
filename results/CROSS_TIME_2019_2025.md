## ⭐ THE RESULT: machine-consciousness discourse did NOT increase

LaMDA/Lemoine was **June 2022**. ChatGPT was **November 2022**. This comparison spans that entire transition, 32,000 classified documents.

- **Denial: zero → zero.** Exact, with CIs of `[0, 0]`.
- **Consciousness-as-topic: 0.1515% → 0.1554%.** Unchanged.
- **Affirmation: 1 document → 3 documents.** CI includes zero.

**This is not the "2019 was too early" story.** It is the stronger claim: the AI-consciousness discourse explosion everyone assumes happened **does not appear in web-scale crawled text**, three years after ChatGPT.

## ⚠️ HONEST CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER CLAIM

At 04:55 I reported the cross-time control as **passed**, on the basis of the S− stratum alone (P: 0.258% → 0.220%).

**Weighted across both strata the picture is weaker:** P declines **0.347% → 0.246%** (−29%), and **P+Q declines 0.797% → 0.418%** (−48%). The confidence intervals barely overlap.

**The correct statement is "roughly stable," not "stable."** The pipeline is not drifting wildly, and the P baseline still moves far less than a category going from present to absent would — but I framed it more favourably than the weighted numbers support, using the subset that looked better. **Recorded here rather than quietly amended.**

*(Whether the P/Q decline is real — web text shifting commercial/SEO over six years — or a crawl-construction artefact is not resolvable from this data. It is a limitation, not a finding.)*

## ⚠️ THE APERTURE OBJECTION, AND WHY IT SHARPENS RATHER THAN WEAKENS

**The objection:** AI-consciousness discourse concentrates on Twitter/X and Reddit. Common Crawl substantially under-samples both (login walls, robots.txt). So the discourse may exist in venues this corpus cannot see.

**That is true, and it does not rescue the deflationary claim.**

> **FineWeb *is* the training-corpus type.** If the discourse lives in venues web crawls miss, then it is **also missing from the training data** — and it still cannot explain training-derived behaviour.
>
> **"It's on Twitter" is not a defence when Twitter is not in the corpus either.**

The claim under test is specifically about *what models were trained on*. Measuring the crawled web is measuring the right thing.

## What this licenses, and what it does not

✅ **Licensed:** the *saturation* premise of the deflationary claim is false, in both directions, in both years, for the corpus type models are actually trained on.

❌ **Not licensed:** that models cannot have learned it. **0.04% of 15T tokens is still ~6 billion tokens.** Rare as a fraction ≠ absent in volume.

❌ **Not licensed:** anything about whether any system is conscious. This removes one lazy argument (see `HANDOFF_WRITE_THE_PAPER.md` §7).
